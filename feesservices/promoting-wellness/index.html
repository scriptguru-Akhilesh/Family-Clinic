Promoting wellness

Care for every age. Support at every stage.

Comprehensive health screening is provided by our clinic doctors. We call it an MOT as it is an all over wellness check and a reminder that while you regularly service your car – you should do the same courtesy for your body!

Our screening consists of a full medical, ECG ( heart tracing) and full blood tests to check cholesterol, iron levels, thyroid levels, liver and kidney function, diabetes screening etc.

Our list of tests is tailored to the individuals’ needs and age.
